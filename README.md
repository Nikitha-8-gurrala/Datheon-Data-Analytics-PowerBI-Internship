## Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis on Superstore Retail Sales

Analyzed retail sales data to identify sales trends, profitability, customer behavior, and discount patterns.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Google Colab.

[View Task 1 Notebook](Datheon_Task1_Superstore_EDA.ipynb)

## Task 2: Interactive Power BI Executive Sales & Profit Dashboard

Developed an interactive dashboard to monitor sales, profit, regional performance, and product profitability.

**Key Findings:**
- Total Sales: 2.30M
- Total Profit: 286.40K
- Profit Margin: 12.47%

[View Task 2 Project](Task-2-PowerBI-Dashboard/)

## Task 3: Customer Cohort & Churn Analytics

Analyzed the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset to identify retention patterns, high-risk customer segments, and customer churn factors.

**Key Findings:**
- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churn Rate: 26.54%
- Retention Rate: 73.46%

Tenure-based retention proportions are used because historical cohort data is unavailable.

[View Task 3 Project](Task-3-Customer-Churn-Analytics/)

## Task 4: Corporate Financial & Margin Performance Dashboard

Developed an interactive financial dashboard featuring income statement analysis, quarterly performance, business segment profitability, and simulated budget variance analysis.

**Key Findings:**
- Gross Sales: $127.93M
- Net Sales: $118.73M
- Gross Profit: $16.89M
- Gross Profit Margin: 14.23%

The simulated budget assumes 10% growth over previous-year sales. Gross profit margin is used because operating expense data is unavailable.

[View Task 4 Project](Task-4-Corporate-Financial-Dashboard/)
